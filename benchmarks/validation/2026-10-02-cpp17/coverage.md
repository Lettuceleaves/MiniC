Scope is the selected Windows x64 LLP64 algorithm profile, not full ISO C++17, every standard-header overload, or a page-by-page claim for the user's PDF. UCRT conversions use their independent default-C locale; native C `strtod` retains its MSVCRT contract. Operation-count and representation checks are separate from timing/performance claims; this export contains no formal benchmark result.
